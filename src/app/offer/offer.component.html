What we offer

After-school judo clubs built for primary schools.

We deliver high-quality after-school judo sessions in primary schools across Belfast. Each club includes:

  • Weekly sessions during term time
  • Professional instruction by a qualified national coach
  • All judo mats provided
  • Sessions adapted to age and ability
  • Emphasis on safety, inclusivity and fun

Our goal is to provide a positive first experience of judo, laying the foundation for lifelong physical activity, confidence and character.
